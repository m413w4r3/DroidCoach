HEARTBEAT / STALE-SOCKET CORRECTION
- Connector: responder handshake timeout (default 8 s) closes a relay data
  socket that never receives a fresh e2ee_hello after a connector restart.
  Test: integration/relay/pc/test/stale-client-timeout.test.ts PASS.
- Android: RelayBackendTransport sends an application ping every 15 s
  (8 s timeout) and reconnects on missing pong. The instrumented tests use a
  3 s/2 s cadence. JVM tests:
  heartbeatReconnectsWhenThePeerStopsAnswering PASS,
  heartbeatPongKeepsAHealthySessionConnected PASS.
- Without these, a client whose connector died could remain attached to the
  relay with a dead Noise session and never reconnect.
